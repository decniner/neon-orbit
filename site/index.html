PILOT'S FIELD NOTES

Make space.
Stay alive.

Fly between the illuminated left and right corridor rails. Strafe with A / D or ← / →; hold W / ↑ to move forward and S / ↓ to move backward. Your guns stay pointed up-route. On a touch screen, drag the left stick to steer.

Press Space or the dash button to evade danger and deploy a holographic decoy. It draws enemy gunfire and guided missiles for 2.4 seconds and can absorb three hits; the dash and decoy recharge together in three seconds.

Collect the mint energy left by defeated enemies. Collect again within three seconds to build a multiplier, up to 8×. Gold W grants rapid triple fire, cyan P grants a piercing pulse cannon, and violet S grants homing seeker missiles. Each weapon lasts 12 seconds. Green + pickups restore one hull point. Supplies first arrive after four seconds, then every twelve seconds.

Travel through Lunar Relay, Ashen Nebula, and Void Citadel. After each 42-second approach, fight its guardian to unlock the next destination. Gather energy to evolve your ship: weapon pods, armor, hotter engines, a salvage field, and stronger dash hardware. The route gets tougher as stages repeat. Press P / Escape to pause and M to toggle sound. Switching tabs pauses the game automatically.
